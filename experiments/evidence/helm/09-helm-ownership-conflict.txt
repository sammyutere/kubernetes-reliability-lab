Helm installation initially failed because Kubernetes resources already existed and were managed by kubectl rather than Helm.

Resources deleted before successful Helm install:

- NetworkPolicy
- HorizontalPodAutoscaler
- PodDisruptionBudget
- Service
- Deployment
- ConfigMap

Secret resource intentionally preserved.

Operational lesson:
Helm does not automatically adopt existing kubectl-managed resources unless ownership metadata matches the Helm release.
